Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

100pushups is operated by Tobias Junsten. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@junsten.se.

Your information at a glance

We save your pushup progress so you can track previous days and streaks. Joining a private group shares your display name, progress and confirmed-set activity with that group. You can delete your account and associated data from Account settings in the app.

Information the service uses

  • Account information: an automatically generated Firebase account ID and the display name you choose. No email address or password is required to use the app. An anonymous account is still a persistent identifier; its associated data is not anonymized.
  • Fitness progress: confirmed pushup sets, daily dates and totals, goal-completion information, and timestamps. The app uses your local date and timezone offset to assign progress to a day. History and streaks are calculated from saved daily totals.
  • Group information: group names, ownership, memberships, invitation codes and confirmed-set activity, including the member's display name, set amount and daily total.
  • Notification information: device registration tokens, Firebase installation identifiers, device platform, and delivery records needed to send group alerts and handle invalid registrations.
  • Technical information: network requests and service logs used to operate and secure the backend. Our infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, device/browser information, request timestamps and service diagnostics.
  • Support correspondence: your email address, message and any information you choose to include when contacting support. Please do not send private invitation codes or authentication tokens.

The app does not request access to HealthKit, your address book, photos, microphone or device location. Its current version does not include advertising or a third-party analytics SDK. The public privacy and support pages do not add analytics or advertising scripts.

Why we use this information

We use this information to provide daily tracking and history, calculate streaks, manage private groups and invitations, deliver requested notifications, respond to support requests, prevent duplicate set submissions, process deletion requests, and maintain the service's security and reliability.

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on providing the service you request and our legitimate interests in operating and protecting it. Optional push notifications are controlled through your device permission, which you can withdraw in your device settings.

What other group members can see

Members of a group can see the group's name, members' display names, shared daily progress and confirmed-set activity. Group alerts can show a teammate's display name, set amount and total on a recipient's lock screen. We do not make groups publicly searchable.

Invitation codes and links can be reused while active. Share them only with people you intend to invite. A group creator can revoke the active invitation.

Service providers and processing locations

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Hosting to operate the service. Apple Push Notification service delivers iPhone alerts; Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers Android alerts. GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages. Support email is handled through the operator's email service.

The application's primary Firestore database and backend Functions are configured in Belgium (europe-west1). This does not mean all information stays in Belgium: authentication, notification delivery, hosting, security logs and support services may involve processing in other countries, including the United States.

See Firebase's privacy information, Apple's privacy policy, and GitHub's privacy statement for provider practices. Service providers process information needed to deliver their services. We do not use the app's data for targeted advertising or sell it to data brokers.

Storage and retention

Your account, progress and group records are kept to provide the service until you request deletion. A new day does not erase your previous totals. Support correspondence is retained to resolve and follow up your request; you can contact us to request its deletion.

Technical logs, service data and provider backups follow the relevant providers' retention schedules. Removing live application records does not promise immediate removal of every provider backup or operational log; details are available in the provider privacy information linked above.

Delete your account and data

  1. In the app, open Groups or History.
  2. Tap the settings icon to open Account settings.
  3. Select Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion removes your profile, private progress, device-token records, memberships, shared activity, related set-operation and delivery records, and Firebase Authentication identity. Empty groups are removed. If other active members remain in a group you own, ownership transfers to a remaining member, the group is renamed “Pushup group”, and its active invitation is revoked. Other members keep their own progress.

Deletion runs in the background and normally completes within a few minutes. Temporary failures are retried. The app shows progress and completion, and does not automatically create a replacement account. A non-identifying receipt containing only deletion status and a timestamp may remain; it contains no account ID, display name, group identifiers, device tokens or fitness data.

Previously delivered notifications and copies that other people have made cannot be recalled. Deleting the app alone does not request server-side account deletion. If you have lost access to your anonymous account, contact us; we may need information to verify ownership before handling a request.

Your choices and rights

You can choose a display name that does not identify you, decide whether to join a group, and deny or disable notifications without losing access to group progress. Anonymous sign-in does not provide a recoverable email/password account; losing the saved session can make previous data inaccessible.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete or restrict processing of your information, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact info@junsten.se to make a request. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Changes and contact

We update this notice as the service changes. The date at the top identifies the current version. For questions, privacy requests or concerns, contact Tobias Junsten at info@junsten.se.

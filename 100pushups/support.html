Help and support

100pushups helps you track daily pushups and encourage teammates in private groups.

Contact us

Contact Tobias Junsten at info@junsten.se for help or privacy requests. You can copy the email address if a mail app is not configured.

For a bug report, include your iPhone model, iOS/app version, what happened, and what you expected. A screenshot can help. Do not include authentication tokens or private invitation codes.

Logging pushups

Add pushups to your draft set on Today, then tap Lock in. Your total is confirmed after the server accepts it. Draft additions alone do not update group activity or send alerts.

Use Edit total to correct today's total. Corrections do not create set activity or notify teammates.

History and streaks

History shows previous saved daily totals, goal status, and current/best streaks. Reaching 100 pushups completes a day. An unfinished today leaves yesterday's streak active until the day ends. Older on-device totals from before cloud tracking were introduced are not automatically imported.

Joining a private group

Ask the creator for an invitation link or code. Open Groups → Join by code to enter the code manually. An expired, revoked or incorrect invitation will not let you join; ask the creator for a new one.

Notifications

Open Groups → Enable alerts. If permission was denied, enable notifications for 100pushups in iPhone Settings, return to Groups and try again. Each confirmed set alerts other current group members; the first crossing of 100 has celebration copy. Your own sets should not alert you.

Notifications are best-effort. Check the group activity feed for confirmed sets. Notification delivery also depends on Apple, your connection and device notification settings.

Connection or loading problems

Check your connection and use the screen's Retry option. A set with an uncertain outcome keeps its request ID so a retry can confirm it without counting it twice. After relaunch, the app checks pending sets before loading your total.

Account access

The app uses an anonymous account and a display name. There is no password to reset. Reinstalling the app or losing its saved session may make the previous account inaccessible. If this happens, email us; please do not assume a new display name will restore an old account.

Delete your account

Open Groups or History, tap the settings icon, then choose Delete my account and confirm. You can also open Account settings during onboarding. You do not need to email support to initiate deletion.

Deletion normally completes within a few minutes and continues if you close the app. The app shows the request's status when reopened. Temporary failures are retried automatically. Existing progress cannot be restored after deletion. See the privacy policy for exactly what is removed and how owned groups are handled.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, export or other privacy requests, email info@junsten.se. Describe the request without including private credentials. We may need to verify account ownership.
